---
title: Activate your licence
description: Activate a Desktop or Enterprise licence in the extension
---

Business use of the extension needs a licence: see [licensing](/docs/cheerpj-jnlp-runner/licensing). There are two ways to activate one:

- **Licence key**, for a Desktop licence bought online, for 1 to 20 desktops. See [activate with a licence key](#activate-with-a-licence-key).
- **Licence token**, for technical evaluations and Enterprise licences. A token activates offline, with no contact with our licence server. See [activate offline with a licence token](#activate-offline-with-a-licence-token).

With an Enterprise licence, desktop administrators can also set the token for every desktop by policy, so users activate nothing. See [configure the extension for enterprises](/docs/cheerpj-jnlp-runner/guides/configure-extension-for-enterprises#licensetoken).

## Activate with a licence key

### Buy a licence key

Click _**Manage License**_ in the extension's popup, or in the side panel next to a running application.

The licence screen opens, where you enter a key or a token. If you don't have a key yet, click the link to buy one.

<div class="mx-24">![](/assets/jnlp-screenshots/license_key_annotated.png)</div>

### Enter the key

The link opens the [Leaning Technologies shop](/payment-jnlp), where you buy a Desktop licence for 1 to 20 desktops.

<div class="mx-24">![](/assets/jnlp-screenshots/purchase_license.png)</div>

Once you have paid, the shop shows your licence key and emails it to you. Copy the key, go back to the extension and click _**Enter License**_.

<div class="mx-24">![](/assets/jnlp-screenshots/enter_license.png)</div>

With _**Key**_ selected, paste the key into the field.

<div class="mx-24">![](/assets/jnlp-screenshots/license_key.png)</div>

Click _**Validate**_. The extension checks the key with our licence server, and shows a message once it is valid.

<div class="mx-24">![](/assets/jnlp-screenshots/valid_license.png)</div>

## Activate offline with a licence token

We send you a licence token for a technical evaluation or an Enterprise licence. Click _**Manage License**_, then _**Enter License**_. Select _**Token**_, paste the token and click _**Validate**_. The extension checks the token itself, with no contact with our licence server.

<div class="mx-24">![](/assets/jnlp-screenshots/license_token.png)</div>
